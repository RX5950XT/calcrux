# CalcRux 隱私權政策 / Privacy Policy

最後更新：2026-10-19

## 繁體中文

CalcRux 不收集、不儲存、不分享任何個人資料。

- 計算歷史、設定與匯率快取只存在你的裝置上，不會上傳。
- 匯率功能會連線到 ExchangeRate-API（`open.er-api.com`）下載公開匯率；這個請求不帶任何個人資料。該服務的伺服器可能依其政策記錄一般連線資訊（例如 IP 位址）。
- 不含廣告、分析或追蹤 SDK，也不需要登入。
- 權限：網路（下載匯率）、震動（按鍵回饋）。

如有疑問請到 <https://github.com/RX5950XT/calcrux/issues> 提出。

## English

CalcRux does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- Calculation history, settings, and cached exchange rates stay on your device and are never uploaded.
- The currency feature downloads public exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API (`open.er-api.com`); the request contains no personal data. That service may log standard connection information (such as IP address) under its own policy.
- No ads, analytics, or tracking SDKs. No sign-in required.
- Permissions: Internet (download exchange rates), Vibrate (key feedback).

Questions: <https://github.com/RX5950XT/calcrux/issues>
